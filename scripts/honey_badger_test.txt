What if one of the toughest predators in Africa could survive an attack from one of nature's most dangerous weapons?

The honey badger is famous for its incredible toughness. But its real advantage is not just strength. It is a combination of thick skin, powerful jaws, sharp claws, and an extraordinary ability to keep fighting when things go terribly wrong.

---
IMAGE: 002.jpg
The honey badger can look surprisingly small compared with many of the animals it challenges.

But size can be misleading.

Its body is built for conflict. Powerful shoulders, strong forelimbs, and long claws help it dig, tear, and defend itself.

And then there is its skin.

The honey badger's loose skin can move around its body, making it surprisingly difficult for an attacker to hold it securely.

---
IMAGE: 003.jpg
But the most dangerous situation comes when the honey badger attacks venomous prey.

Snakes are part of its diet, including highly venomous species.

That creates a terrifying problem.

The snake has venom.

The honey badger has to get close enough to bite it.

So how does this small mammal survive a battle where one mistake could be fatal?

That is where the honey badger becomes truly extraordinary.
